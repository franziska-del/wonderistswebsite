The experience

Step out of the routine.
Side by side.

Step away from the usual routine and take on new challenges side by side. Get curious, try things you haven’t done before, practise the skills most useful for the future, meet interesting people and create the kind of stories your kids will still remember years from now.

Across three days and two nights, families share outdoor challenges and moments of discovery—with dedicated time to reflect, bond and build new connections together.

The itinerary

Three days. One story
you’ll keep telling.

The pace moves from arrival and adventure, through a full day of practical challenge and shared creativity, to reflection and one last hit of the coast.

01 Arrive & reconnect 02 Learn & make 03 Carry it home
01

Day one

Arrive & reconnect

Meet in Murwillumbah, get moving through the landscape, then settle into Coolamon Station for the first evening together.

Bike rideGlampingCampfire
  1. 01

    Northern Rivers Rail Trail

    A relaxed ride through the landscape, with either lunch and a swim at Hosanna Farm or a longer ride towards Burringbar and a picnic before returning to Murwillumbah.

  2. 02

    Settle into Coolamon Station

    Drive to the property and make a home for the weekend in private glamping tents.

  3. 03

    Critters after dark

    Take a torch-lit walk around the property and see what comes alive once the sun goes down.

  4. 04

    Stories around the fire

    Ben shares what leading and learning through failure looked like on his 2019 Arctic expedition, followed by an ice breaker and shared campfire dinner.

02

Day two

Learn & make

A full day of practical skills, teamwork, food, performance and a sky big enough to get wonderfully lost in.

NavigationKup MurriStargazing
  1. 01

    Sunrise & breakfast

    Start with a sunrise walk before coming together for breakfast.

  2. 02

    Prepare a Kup Murri

    Work together to prepare a traditional earth-oven feast, then leave it to slow-cook through the day.

  3. 03

    Outdoor skills with Innes

    Build confidence through team building, rogaining, fire building and navigation. Innes is a trained teacher with a Master of Arts in Outdoor Education who brings the activities to life for kids of all ages.

  4. 04

    Unearth & share

    Come back together to uncover the Kup Murri and share the result of the morning’s teamwork.

  5. 05

    Bush theatre

    Family teams create and present a short performance. Each one is filmed and gifted back as a record of the time together.

  6. 06

    Byron Bay Stargazing

    Explore stars, planets and the stories behind the constellations through a hands-on astronomy experience with expert guides.

03

Day three

Carry it home

Slow down, name what mattered, make one shared commitment—and finish the weekend in the water.

AnimalsReflectSnorkel or surf
  1. 01

    Farmyard morning

    Join the Coolamon Station farmer for animal feeding and egg collecting after breakfast.

  2. 02

    A shared commitment

    Each parent-child pair reflects on the weekend—what happened, what surprised them and what they want their relationship to look like going forward—then chooses one commitment to carry home.

  3. 03

    Rock & red thread

    Paint a word or image that represents the commitment, then tie a red thread around each other’s wrist as a physical reminder.

  4. 04

    One last adventure

    After leaving Coolamon Station, close the weekend by snorkelling among turtles at Cook Island Marine Reserve or catching a wave with a surf lesson.

Who is Next Gen for?

Entrepreneurial parents and the next generation who want to step away from routine, try unfamiliar things and spend meaningful time together.

Where does it happen?

The experience begins in Murwillumbah and is based at Coolamon Station in the Northern Rivers hinterland, with a final coastal activity at Cook Island or a local surf school.

What is included in the experience?

The planned itinerary spans three days and two nights, including private glamping accommodation and the activities described above. Final inclusions will be shared when dates are confirmed.
